C. Initial GeoGuard rule catalogue — sensor and method selected by use case

R01 · Lake water spread

Sentinel-2 10 m + Sentinel-1 SAR; MNDWI/NDWI and SAR water classification; compare seasonal time series against lake asset context.

R02/R03 · FTL & buffer construction

Commercial 0.3–0.5 m preferred; 1 m screening. Building segmentation/change detection, then intersect with the approved, versioned FTL/buffer polygon.

R04 · Land filling / dumping

10 m BSI for large-area screening; 0.3–1 m commercial imagery for local confirmation; persistent change and field/elevation checks where needed.

R05 · Government parcel occupation

Sentinel broad screening; 0.3–1 m commercial confirmation; building/land-cover change intersected with authenticated survey parcels.

R06 · Vegetation / tree removal

Sentinel-2 10 m NDVI time series for broad change; commercial 0.3–1 m imagery for individual tree/patch review where feasible.

R07 · Levelling / excavation

Commercial stereo or drone survey with DEM/DSM differencing; optical imagery alone indicates surface change, not reliable cut/fill volume.

R08/R09 · Nala, feeder & road change

Commercial 0.3–1 m for narrow channel obstruction and local roads; Sentinel 10 m only for broad corridor screening.

R10 · Citizen incident verification

Classify report first, then resolve the relevant rule and search the latest suitable imagery. If unavailable, queue targeted acquisition or field inspection.

Rule parameters are configurable and versioned: preferred/accepted GSD, freshness, cloud and viewing quality, minimum mapping unit, model version, confidence thresholds, spatial condition, escalation and fallback. Example cloud thresholds are starting parameters to validate, not universal constants.

Suitability and evidence guardrail: Select the newest suitable observation, not simply the newest scene. Keep image suitability, AI detection and spatial/legal-boundary match confidence separate. A coarse, stale, cloud-obscured or unlicensed image must not produce a “no change” conclusion.
Decision boundary: AI and GIS identify potential changes and spatial overlaps. They do not independently determine legal encroachment. Legal interpretation, boundary approval and enforcement decisions remain with authorised government officers.

Key architectural principles

Event-driven, asynchronous processing · Modular backend before microservices sprawl · One asset registry · Separate original evidence from derived analysis · Latest valid image, not merely latest scene · Legal conclusions reserved for authorised officials · Pilot sized for 10–15 lakes and expandable.

Architecture is a logical reference design. Specific government hosting services, external API permissions, imagery licences, data-sharing agreements, security controls and final capacity must be validated during discovery and procurement. Sentinel revisit is not continuous monitoring; citizen-triggered analysis uses the latest suitable available observation or routes the case for field / higher-resolution verification.
