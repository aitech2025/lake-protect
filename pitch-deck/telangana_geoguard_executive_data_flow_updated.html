Three imagery tiers: screen, confirm, verify

Screen, every asset weekly: Sentinel-2 at 10 m and Sentinel-1 radar for soil fill, clearing, water context and built-up change of 1,000 sq m or more (radar-confirmed). Confirm, priority cases only: 0.3–0.5 m commercial tasking or ISRO Cartosat-3 through NRSC, for buildings, sheds, nala obstruction and small dumps. Verify: field team with geotagged photos, plus CCTV and drones where they exist; stereo or drone elevation for cut-and-fill. Sheds and walls under 10 m are never claimed from Sentinel.

Fail-safe: if no suitable observation exists, the asset is reported as not observed and a sharper-imagery or field task is created. A coarse, stale or cloud-obscured image is never treated as proof that no change occurred.

Shared platform backbone: connects every stage

One permanent asset ID links imagery, boundaries, legal records, citizen reports, detections, alerts, notifications and official actions. A modular FastAPI application with a PostgreSQL outbox and one scheduled worker moves work between components; a message broker, workflow engine or Kubernetes is added only when measured load needs it. PostgreSQL/PostGIS and governed object storage hold the authoritative records, and alerts keep working when imagery processing is down.

Government ownership & control

The State owns hosting, data, identity, keys, boundary approvals, officer decisions and actions. Owner names appear only after a field check confirms a change, to authorised roles, and every view is logged.

Security, privacy & resilience

WAF and firewall, State SSO with MFA, role and area scope inside the database, hash-chained audit, single-case downloads, DPDP Act minimisation for reporters, backup and disaster recovery.

Technology foundation

Python / FastAPI · PostgreSQL 16 / PostGIS 3.4 · S3-compatible storage · outbox + worker · React / MapLibre PWA · rasterio · Docker, then Kubernetes when ready.

Important: GeoGuard flags physical changes and spatial overlaps for authorised review. It does not decide legality, and only confirmed boundaries lead to action. Satellite availability is periodic, not continuous; every alert and asset shows its last clear look.
