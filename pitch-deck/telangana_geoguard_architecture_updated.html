Government geospatial intelligence platform

Telangana GeoGuard

End-to-end logical and physical architecture: from satellite, government and citizen sources, through change detection and an automatic legal check, to field verification, sealed evidence and the officer's decision. Each component is marked with what runs today and what comes next.
Government-hosted · Pilot deployment · Joint operations
Running today on real data: 99 Hyderabad lakes inside the ORR screened from Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-1 with images up to 22 Nov 2026: 7,652 clear looks, every lake seen clearly within the last 14 days, 58 candidate changes on 15 lakes. All 58 sit on the watch list because these lake outlines are derived, not notified. The officer loop (legal check, field app, evidence pack, monthly page) runs end to end, offline, on a labelled fictional demo world. No accuracy is claimed until the blind backtest on 30 public cases.

R10 · Citizen report verification

Match the report to an asset, re-check the latest suitable look; if there is none, queue sharper imagery or a field visit.

False-alarm controls, all required (built): persistence over two looks at least 5 days apart with no reversal · minimum area 1,000 sq m · same season in the two previous years · drought (worse than the 5 nearest lakes by 10 points) · baseline date · radar confirmation for built-up. Designed: radar-only alerts in the monsoon marked "optical confirmation pending". Thresholds are starting values, tuned on separate lakes and frozen before the blind backtest.

Suitability guardrail: select the newest suitable observation, not simply the newest scene. Keep image suitability, change detection and boundary-match confidence separate. A coarse, stale, cloud-obscured or unlicensed image never produces a "no change" result: the asset is reported as not observed instead.
Boundary status gates action: only a final notification or a physically marked boundary can lead to action (cohorts A and B). A preliminary boundary allows a field check only (C). A derived outline is watch-only (D). About 23% of HMDA lakes (765 of 3,342) have a final FTL notice, which is why boundary status is shown on every alert.
Decision boundary: the system reports physical change and spatial overlap. It never labels a change illegal and never recommends demolition; legal interpretation, boundary approval and action stay with authorised officers. Detection is owner-blind: owner names appear only after a field check confirms the change, only to nodal, revenue and legal roles, and every view is logged.

Cross-cutting governance

Boundary status on every alert · Baseline date: change first seen after 19 Jul 2024 · Owner-blind detection · Source, licence and date on every record (Copernicus attribution; commercial licences must allow use in court) · Section 63(4) Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam certificate for electronic evidence · DPDP Act 2023: the Rules were notified in November 2025, and notice, consent and security duties apply from May 2027, during the paid pilot · Drone flights follow the Drone Rules 2021 airspace map: zones near operational airports need air traffic control permission · Single-case downloads, no bulk export · Two-person closure · Hash-chained audit · Wording: "change", never "violation".

✓

Key architectural principles

Modular monolith with an outbox first; brokers, Kubernetes or microservices only on measured need · One asset registry · Process each scene once, match every asset · Latest valid look, never merely the latest scene · Only confirmed boundaries lead to action · Officers decide · Each phase earns the next: backtest (Dec 2026), demonstration on about 20 recovered assets (Jan–Mar 2027), paid pilot of 100 lakes and 10 parcels through one monsoon (Apr–Sep 2027), core urban region of 900+ lakes (from Oct 2027).

Architecture is a logical reference design. Specific government hosting services, external API permissions, imagery licences, data-sharing agreements, security controls and final capacity must be validated during discovery and procurement. Sentinel revisit is not continuous monitoring: an asset is only as current as its last clear look, which every alert and asset page shows. Citizen-triggered analysis uses the latest suitable observation or routes the case for sharper imagery or a field visit. "Built" means running in the prototype: detection on real Sentinel data for 99 Hyderabad lakes, and the officer loop on a labelled fictional demo world.
