Rule-driven imagery strategy — match the sensor to the question

10 m Sentinel-1/2: lake water spread, broad vegetation and large-area land-cover screening. 0.3–1 m commercial optical: construction, FTL/buffer structures, parcel occupation, nala obstruction and small-area dumping confirmation. Commercial stereo / drone elevation: cut-fill, excavation and land-levelling assessment. Each rule specifies preferred/accepted resolution, freshness, cloud/viewing quality, model, spatial test, confidence and fallback.

Fail-safe: if no suitable observation exists, create a pending-imagery or field-verification task. Never treat a coarse, stale or cloud-obscured image as proof that no change occurred.

Shared platform backbone — connects every stage

Common asset ID links imagery, legal records, boundaries, citizen reports, AI detections, incidents, notifications and official actions. Event-driven integration (RabbitMQ + Temporal) moves work between components; PostgreSQL/PostGIS and governed object storage preserve the authoritative records.
